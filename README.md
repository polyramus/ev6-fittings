# ev6-fittings

*Ordning och reda* for the Kia EV6 — Swedish for “order and tidiness,” which is
the entire product line in one phrase.

3D-printed interior utility parts, one at a time. Each item fixes a small,
daily friction in the car that no official accessory covers. One subdirectory
per item; measured source data (STLs, photos) lives in `data/`.

## Items

### Cup-holder inlay (`cup-holder-inlay/`)

Drops into the center console cup holders:

- kills the rattle from the factory holder springs
- gives slim bottles a snug, non-rocking fit
